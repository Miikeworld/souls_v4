The pelvis solve: correction = floor under the deeper foot (per-foot `SphereCast` probes, `GroundingSolver.ProbeFootGround`) minus that foot's calibrated sole bottom — `ankle − soleOffset` per side, soleOffset from `GroundingSolver.MeasureSoleOffset` at Start (baked-mesh 12.5cm ankle column; avatar feetBottomHeight is only a fallback), MIN'd with the live toe joint minus a fixed 2cm pad (`SoleEstimate` — toes are NOT calibrated per-pose, which was the old trap: a toe pad measured in a raised-toe pose reads wrong permanently; the fixed pad only needs the joint's live position. A fixed vertical offset under-reads a pitched foot by ~9cm at toe-off and the solve answered that phantom gap by sinking the pelvis — the locomotion sink on the new protagonist rig). Enemies ground by sole-near-floor, the player gates on `isGrounded` + planar speed — and the anti-sink floor is a CONTINUOUS lower bound on the target (`SoleFloor`: the target may never leave the estimated sole under floor+5mm, in the active AND released/decaying paths) — a steady pin, not a trigger; the earlier snap-on-trigger ping-ponged at its boundary (the per-frame buzz). Snap only fires for confirmed landings and real authored dips >6cm. The chase runs only on CONTACT — both soles above their floors = mid-stride flight, hold the offset (otherwise the correction sags into every stride); a sustained >0.25s no-contact pose is a floating clip and pulls down again. The player speed gate latches with hysteresis (release >maxSpeed*1.25, re-engage <maxSpeed*0.8) so the walk/run boundary can't toggle the solve per frame. A `bounds.min.y` dip guard lifts the target only when a sole is within 35cm of its probed floor — on airborne attack flares the AABB reads a swept limb and would levitate the planted foot. The guard subtracts `meshSlack` (calibrated AABB overhang below the sole line at spawn) — without it the padded bounds parked the soles `slack − 2cm` above the floor, the constant slight float. Dead bodies ground by lowest body bone; `IsDisplacing`/`IsRooted` (slide/roll/wallrun) release everything, grounded attacks AND grounded slides keep the solve (a slide whose capsule is grounded is the same standing-pose problem — releasing exposes the authored hip height, which floats on rigs whose slide clip doesn't plant the soles).
