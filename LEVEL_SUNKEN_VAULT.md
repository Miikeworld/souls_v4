# Sunken Vault: story level design (working title)

This level replaces Foundry Depths. Scene: `Assets/_Project/Scenes/03_SunkenVault.unity`, generated by
`Tools > Project Restart > Level > Build Sunken Vault` (run outside Play Mode). It needs `00_TestBlockout` with its dressed skeletons.

- Review shots: `Level > Capture Sunken Vault Views` writes them to `Tools/LevelReview/SunkenVault/`.
- Top-down map: `Tools/LevelReview/SunkenVault_Map.png`.
- The main menu's NEW GAME and CONTINUE load this scene once it is in Build Settings. Otherwise they fall back to `00_TestBlockout`.

"Sunken Vault", "The Swollen King", "Vault Captain" and the other zone and enemy names are descriptive placeholders, following STORY.md's naming rule.

## Story purpose

This was the hold where Corestone was cut and stored, built with PolygonDungeonRealms' Dungeon_01 dwarf-vault kit. The king kept the
Crimson stock under his own hall because he trusted no one else with it. The Crimson never left; it went into him. STORY.md's canon
says Crimson exposure changes people physically, and the king is the extreme case: a giant still guarding a hold that died around him.
The level keeps the visual rule:
- Violet marks controlled power: rune bands in the gate plaza and the wall-run markings.
- Crimson marks overload: the cathedral's core growth, the throne-hall runes, the king's enrage and his shockwaves.

## Crowd combat (beat-'em-up when the enemies are weak)

- **Turns:** at most two enemies hold melee tokens, per spec §252. Everyone else circles on a ~3.6 m ring and spreads out by
  separation steering instead of stacking. One enemy noticing you alerts its pack.
- **Moves:**
  - slash;
  - two-hit combo;
  - heavy: a long telegraph with a red glint, 1.8× damage;
  - lunge: a gap-closer from 3–6.5 m.

  Each windup stops tracking partway through, so a dodge that reads the swing works.
- **Knockdowns:** a finisher hit or a launcher landing knocks trash down (the knockdown clip carries the body back, then it gets up).
  Elites and posture-metered enemies keep flinch and poise.
- **Player:** crowd aim assist bends swings onto the enemy you steer toward (60° cone, 4.5 m). With no input and no lock, you
  swing at the nearest enemy in front. It only applies at swing start, never mid-contact. Reach is now measured to the enemy's body
  surface, so big enemies are hittable from blade range.

## The Swollen King (hard boss, giant-camera fight)

The DungeonRealms dwarf king scaled to 5.6 m, with a large war hammer. `BossColossus` brain, `ColossusBase.controller`. 2600 HP, poise 140.

- **Phase 1:**
  - Sweep: a 200° arc.
  - Smash: overhead, plus a **shockwave ring**. Jump it or dodge through it.
  - Stomp: radial. It punishes standing between his feet.
  - Leap: a gap-closing slam with a landing blast.
- **Phase 2 (60%):** a cinematic roar. A two-window Combo joins, the rings grow, Stomp also throws a ring, and the pace rises.
- **Enraged (25%):** a crimson aura light, 25% shorter cooldowns, and the Smash fires a second delayed ring.
- **Posture break:** he is knocked to the floor for about 3.4 s, then gets up. That is the big punish window.
- **Camera (Genshin / Souls-remake style):**
  - **Intro** (first encounter per session): a low wide reveal, a push up his body, a face close-up on the roar, and a ring burst.
    Retries skip it.
  - **Fight framing:** the orbit pulls back about 1.65×, lifts slightly, and the look target slides toward his upper body,
    weighted by how much you already face him. You keep full camera control.
  - **Phase shot:** over the shoulder with a slight orbit during the roar.
  - **Death:** slowed world and a wide orbit as he falls.

  The player is held and invulnerable only while a shot owns the screen.
- A defeated boss stays dead through rests and deaths.

## Art and tech

- **Kit:** Dungeon_01 dwarf modules.
  - Walls are stacked in 5 m tiers: Wall_05 base, Wall_03 middles, Wall_02 top.
  - Pillar_02 at every 10 m joint, Pillar_06 columns, Wall_Archway_03 doors, Floor_21/02/26.
  - Balustrade_03 rails, Rock_Cliff chasm faces, Stairs_01, which the builder orients by measuring which way the mesh rises.
  - Rune symbols as glowing bands, gold, statues, chandeliers, banners, the throne, crystals.
  - FX come from PolygonParticles.
- **Shader:** every static mesh goes through **Mechanical Environment**. Lava, Corestone, runes, wall-run markings and the fog
  wall are project-owned Unlit materials (`ArtDirection/Materials/SunkenVault`). Vendor assets are untouched; DungeonRealms
  characters (boss, NPC) re-shade to URP at runtime.
- **Collision:** invisible boxes and ramps on the design grid. Art never collides.

  - boss hammer grip, which is socketed at identity and may need a hand-tuned local transform;
  - boss move windows on the scaled rig, cinematic framing and difficulty.
