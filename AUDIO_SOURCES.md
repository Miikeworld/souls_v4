# Audio sources and first-pass mapping

Kenney RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
Kenney Impact Sounds: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Both packs include Creative Commons Zero (CC0) licenses, preserved in Resources/Audio and source download folders.

Selected original clips:
- Slash: RPG Audio knifeSlice; Swish: knifeSlice2; Draw: drawKnife1.
- Hit: impactMetal_light_000; Slam: impactMetal_heavy_000.
- Jump: impactGeneric_light_000; Land: footstep_concrete_000.

Authored SfxBank entries take precedence. Defaults are deliberately quiet (0.25 gain; heavy cues 0.4). This is a starter palette, not a certified final mix. Aural quality, masking under many contacts, and loudspeaker/headphone balance need listening acceptance. No sounds were taken from the inspiration games.
